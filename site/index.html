Local AI, on your own devices

Five projects built on Tether QVAC: models that run on your laptop or phone, private by default, shared peer-to-peer over Holepunch's Hyperswarm instead of a server.

Wallet Guardian

Explains EVM transactions and signatures before you sign: rules-first scam detection, a guarded local QVAC LLM explanation, and a guard for Tether WDK accounts.

CI Doctor

Finds why CI failed: the first real error, real failure or flaky, and the exact log lines. A local QVAC model explains it, and is only shown if its answer passes checks.

Pocket Tune

Teach a small model your voice from notes, chats or spoken answers: scrubbed, fine-tuned on your own computer or phone, compared, and shared peer-to-peer.

Babel Room

Common threads

  • On-device by default. Speech recognition, translation, fine-tuning and explanations run with QVAC on your own hardware.
  • Rules first, models checked. Wallet Guardian and CI Doctor decide with rules; the local model only explains, and only when its answer passes checks.
  • Peer-to-peer. Babel Room rooms and qvac-seed sharing go over Hyperswarm: encrypted, hole-punched, no server.
  • Phones too. Pocket Tune and Babel Room have Expo apps tested on an iPhone 13 Pro Max.
