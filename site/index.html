Teach a small model your voice from notes, chats or spoken answers: scrubbed, fine-tuned on your own computer or phone, compared, and shared peer-to-peer.

Babel Room

Live captions across languages with no server: Whisper and Bergamot on each device, rooms over Hyperswarm, on phones, laptops and terminals.

Stays private: speech becomes text on your device and audio never leaves it. The room code is hashed into a topic, so it never touches the DHT.

Peer-to-peer and private AI

2:58 · the Holepunch stack, layer by layer

Most AI apps send your words, voice and files to someone else's server. These don't. The model runs on your device with QVAC, and when something has to travel, it goes straight to the other person over Holepunch.

Private by default

Speech recognition, translation, fine-tuning and explanations run with QVAC on your own laptop or phone. Your data isn't sent anywhere to be processed.

Peers, not servers

Peers find each other on Hyperswarm by a shared topic. HyperDHT punches through home routers, and every connection is end-to-end encrypted.

Verified, not trusted

Hypercore proves who wrote the bytes; a SHA-256 manifest proves they're the bytes you meant to share. Every peer is treated as a stranger: size-capped, validated and rate-limited.

The stack

  • UDX reliable, ordered streams over UDP
  • HyperDHT finds peers and punches through NATs
  • Hyperswarm joins a topic, opens encrypted connections
  • Hypercore a signed, append-only log
  • Corestore · Hyperdrive many logs, and a P2P file system on top
  • Bare a small JavaScript runtime, so the same code runs on phones

Common threads

  • Rules first, models checked. Wallet Guardian and CI Doctor decide with rules; the local model only explains, and only when its answer passes checks.
  • Tested on the real network. HyperDHT's testnet runs discovery, hole punching and replication locally; Pocket Tune and Babel Room were also tested between peers on the public network.
  • Phones too. Pocket Tune and Babel Room have Expo apps tested on an iPhone 13 Pro Max. On the phone, Babel Room's room runs in a Bare worklet.
